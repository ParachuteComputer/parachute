## Full description (4000 at most)

Parachute keeps your notes, journal, voice notes, people and rooms in a vault that is yours: on a hub you run, one your
org or a friend runs, or Parachute Cloud. Any AI you connect can read it. It stays yours.

What you can do
• Write notes and a daily journal, and find them again.
• Catch a thought fast: a quick note or a voice note, wherever you are.
• Keep the people you know, with far more than a phone's contacts hold.
• Talk in rooms with the people on your Parachute.
• Add applets: small apps that work on your vault, each asking first for what it needs.
• Connect your own AI to your vault, so it answers from your notes.

Private by design
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers. The app keeps nothing about you for us.
• Your data goes only to the Parachute you sign in to, and the relays and services you allow.
• Your key stays in your phone's own key store.
• Open source: github.com/ParachuteComputer/parachute

You need a Parachute to sign in to. Parachute Cloud takes only an email; a hub of your own takes its address.
